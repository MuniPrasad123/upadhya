# Product Vision

Upadhya exists to make a dependable personal learning mentor available to Indian school students across language, location, and income barriers. The product should explain approved curriculum in age-appropriate ways, remain grounded in the learner's textbook, and make the origin of an answer clear.

Success means better understanding—not longer screen time. Product decisions should optimize learning outcomes, accessibility, teacher and parent trust, and student privacy. Generated features must be evaluated before they are described as reliable.

# Privacy and Safety

Upadhya should collect only information required for a learning interaction. Textbooks must come from approved sources and retain provenance. Uploaded PDFs stay in the project-specific runtime directory and are excluded from source control; production storage will require encryption, access control, retention limits, deletion workflows, and audit events.

Children require stronger defaults: no advertising profiles, no sale of learner data, no silent training on private content, age-appropriate explanations, and clear escalation when a request is unsafe or outside the curriculum. Future AI output must cite source pages, separate retrieved facts from generated explanation, and be evaluated for correctness, bias, language quality, and developmental suitability.

Secrets are environment-provided. The example configuration contains only a local placeholder and must not be reused outside local development.
